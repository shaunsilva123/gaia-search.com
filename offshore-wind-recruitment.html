Offshore wind recruitment at Gaia Search

Offshore wind is a key part of what we do at Gaia Search, particularly environmental consultancy, consenting and compliance.

We work with people involved at different stages of offshore wind projects, from the initial surveys and environmental assessments through to securing consent, managing construction requirements and supporting operational wind farms.

We recruit for developers, consultancies and contractors across the UK, covering both offshore projects and their associated onshore infrastructure.

Whether you're considering your next move or looking to bring someone into your team, we're happy to have a chat.

Who we work with

Offshore wind developers, environmental consultancies and contractors across Scotland, England and Wales, supporting projects from early development through to operation.

Offshore wind roles we recruit for

Our recruitment covers the environmental and consenting work involved throughout an offshore wind project's lifecycle.

  1. Surveys & Site Investigation

    Marine ecology, ornithology, marine mammal, benthic and geophysical surveys, including the specialist teams gathering the environmental data needed during project development.

  2. Development & Consenting

    Consents Managers, EIA and HRA specialists, SLVIA consultants, fisheries specialists and stakeholder engagement professionals working on marine licences, Section 36 applications and Development Consent Orders.

  3. Construction & Transmission

    Environmental Managers, Environmental Clerks of Works and Consent Compliance Managers supporting offshore construction, onshore cable routes, substations and grid connections.

  4. Operations & Asset Management

    Environmental and consent compliance specialists working on operational offshore wind farms, including environmental monitoring, reporting and ongoing engagement with regulators.

Frequently asked questions

Do you only recruit for offshore wind?

No. Offshore wind is one of our main areas of recruitment, alongside onshore renewables, environmental consultancy, ecology, EIA, flood risk and sustainability.

What offshore wind roles do you recruit for?

We mainly recruit environmental and consenting professionals, including Consents Managers, EIA and HRA specialists, marine ecologists, ornithologists, Environmental Managers and Consent Compliance Managers.

We also cover related areas such as offshore surveys, transmission and onshore consenting.

Do I need offshore wind experience to move into the sector?

Not always. Some employers are open to people coming from onshore renewables, major infrastructure or environmental consultancy, particularly if they have relevant EIA, consenting or environmental management experience.

Other positions require direct offshore wind experience, especially more senior or specialist roles.

Do you recruit contractors as well as permanent staff?

Yes. We recruit for permanent positions and contract assignments, including specialist consenting and environmental management roles.

Is your recruitment service free for candidates?

Yes. There's no charge for speaking with us, discussing opportunities or applying for a position through Gaia Search.
