Frequently asked questions

Do you only recruit ecologists?

Ecology is one of our core specialisms, alongside EIA, flood risk, offshore wind and the wider environmental consulting and clean energy sectors. Because environmental recruitment is all we do, we understand how ecology fits within multidisciplinary teams.

What levels of ecologist do you recruit?

Every level, from Graduate and Assistant Ecologist through Senior and Principal to Associate, Associate Director and Technical Director, for permanent and contract roles.

Do you recruit licensed ecologists?

Yes. We regularly work with ecologists holding Natural England class and mitigation licences (bats, great crested newts, dormice and more), and we understand how licences, CIEEM membership and survey experience affect a candidate's value.

How much does an ecologist earn in the UK?

It depends on level, location and specialism. As a guide, Senior Ecologists typically earn £38,000 to £45,000 and Principal Ecologists £48,000 to £55,000. See our Ecologist Salary Guide for the full breakdown.

Is it free for candidates?

Yes. Our service is always free for candidates. Employers pay a fee only when they hire through us.
