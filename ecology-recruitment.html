Specialist ecology recruitment

Ecology is one of our main areas of recruitment at Gaia Search. We work exclusively across environmental consulting and clean energy, so we understand the different specialisms within ecology and what employers are looking for.

Whether you're looking to grow your ecology team or considering your next move, we're here to help you find the right fit.

Ecology roles we recruit for

We recruit across all levels of ecology, from graduates starting out in consultancy to experienced technical specialists and directors leading national teams.

  1. Graduate & Assistant Ecologists

    Early-career ecologists looking to develop their survey skills, gain experience across different habitats and work towards protected species licences.

  2. Ecologists & Senior Ecologists

    Ecologists with experience in field surveys, ecological assessments, project management and technical reporting. At Senior level, this often includes managing projects and supporting junior colleagues.

  3. Principal & Associate Ecologists

    Experienced consultants taking the lead on complex projects, EIA ecology chapters, HRAs, licensing strategies and client relationships.

  4. Associate Directors & Technical Directors

    Senior professionals responsible for leading ecology teams, developing client relationships, overseeing technical delivery and helping grow the business.

Frequently asked questions

Do you only recruit ecologists?

No. Ecology is one of our core specialisms, but we also recruit across environmental planning, EIA, flood risk, sustainability, renewable energy and other environmental disciplines.

What levels of ecologist do you recruit?

We recruit at every level, from Graduate and Assistant Ecologists through to Senior, Principal, Associate and Technical Director positions.

Do you recruit licensed ecologists?

Yes. We regularly work with ecologists who hold protected species licences, including bat, great crested newt and dormouse licences. We also recruit specialists in ornithology, botany, aquatic ecology and habitat management.

How much does an ecologist earn in the UK?

In 2026, Graduate Ecologists typically earn around £25,000–£28,000, while Senior Ecologists generally earn £38,000–£45,000. Principal Ecologists typically earn £48,000–£55,000, with salaries increasing at Associate and Director level. You can find a full breakdown in our UK Ecology Salary Guide.

Is your recruitment service free for candidates?

Yes, completely. There's no charge for speaking with us, receiving career advice or applying for roles through Gaia Search.
