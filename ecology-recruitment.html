Ecology recruitment at Gaia Search

Ecology is a big part of what we do at Gaia Search. We specialise in environmental consulting and clean energy recruitment, working with consultancies and other organisations across the UK.

Ecology roles we recruit for

We recruit ecologists at every level, from graduates starting their careers to experienced specialists and directors.

  1. Graduate & Assistant Ecologists

    Early-career ecologists developing their field survey experience, technical skills and working towards protected species licences.

  2. Ecologists & Senior Ecologists

    Ecologists involved in surveys, ecological assessments, technical reporting and project management, including those taking on more responsibility and mentoring junior colleagues.

  3. Principal & Associate Ecologists

    Experienced ecologists leading projects, managing clients and providing technical expertise across EIA, HRA, licensing and habitat management.

  4. Associate Directors & Technical Directors

    Senior ecologists leading teams, overseeing projects, developing client relationships and helping shape the direction of the business.

Frequently asked questions

Do you only recruit ecologists?

No. Ecology is one of our main specialisms, but we also recruit across environmental planning, EIA, flood risk, sustainability and renewable energy.

What levels of ecologist do you recruit?

All levels, from Graduate and Assistant Ecologists through to Senior, Principal, Associate and Technical Director.

Do you recruit licensed ecologists?

Yes. We recruit ecologists with a range of protected species licences, including bats, great crested newts and dormice. We also work with specialists in ornithology, botany, aquatic ecology and habitat management.

How much does an ecologist earn in the UK?

Salaries vary depending on experience, location and specialism. In 2026, Senior Ecologists typically earn around £38,000–£45,000, while Principal Ecologists generally earn £48,000–£55,000. You can find a breakdown of salaries at every level in our 2026 UK Ecology Salary Guide.

Is your recruitment service free for candidates?

Yes. There's no charge for speaking with us, receiving career advice or applying for jobs through Gaia Search.
